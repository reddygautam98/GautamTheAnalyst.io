02Process

How an engagement runs

Clear milestones and check-ins.

  1. 01
    Phase 1 of 4

    Understand

    Discuss the problem, users, data or systems, constraints, deadlines and what success looks like.

    Alignment workshop
  2. 02
    Phase 2 of 4

    Scope

    Agree deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, review points, responsibilities and a practical timeline.

    Scope agreement
  3. 03
    Phase 3 of 4

    Deliver

    Share drafts, analysis or requirements in checkpoints; collect feedback and resolve agreed changes.

    Review checkpoints
  4. 04
    Phase 4 of 4

    Hand over

    Review against acceptance criteria and provide agreed documents, findings or handover notes.

    Deliverable sign-off

04FAQ

Questions before starting

How do fees and timelines work?

They depend on complexity, the output required, dependencies and review cycles. We can agree a discovery phase or a defined deliverable after clarifying the scope. No fixed price is quoted on this site.

Can I start with a small task?

Yes. A requirements review, data-quality check, process walkthrough or feasibility prototype can be a focused first phase when the goals and output are clear.

Can you work with my existing team?

My resume shows work alongside Product, Engineering, QA and business stakeholders. Working cadence and tool access should be agreed before delivery begins.

Can you work with confidential data?

We can discuss an NDA and appropriate handling before sharing project materials. Do not send credentials, regulated records or confidential customer data in the initial enquiry form.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the problem and the outcome you want. We can clarify the scope before deciding on next steps.
