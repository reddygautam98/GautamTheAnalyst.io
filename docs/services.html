02 • Process

How an engagement runs

Clear milestones and check-ins.

  1. 01
    Phase 1 of 4

    Understand

    Discuss the problem, users, data or systems, constraints, deadlines and what success looks like.

    1–2 days • Alignment workshop
    Inspect Discovery Workshop MoM & Decisions →
  2. 02
    Phase 2 of 4

    Scope

    Agree deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, review points, responsibilities and a practical timeline.

    2–4 days • Scope agreement
    Inspect BRD & FRD Specifications →
  3. 03
    Phase 3 of 4

    Deliver

    Share drafts, analysis or requirements in checkpoints; collect feedback and resolve agreed changes.

    1–3 weeks • Review checkpoints
    Inspect RTM & Test Scenarios →
  4. 04
    Phase 4 of 4

    Hand over

    Review against acceptance criteria and provide agreed documents, findings or handover notes.

    2–3 days • Deliverable sign-off
    Inspect Traceability Matrix (RTM) →

04 • FAQ

Questions before starting

How do fees and timelines work?

Fees and timelines depend on complexity, the required deliverables, dependencies and stakeholder review cycles. I can agree a discovery phase or a defined scope after clarifying your goals. For a formal discussion, please use the contact form.

Can I start with a small task?

Yes. A requirements review, data-quality check, process walkthrough or feasibility prototype can be a focused first phase when goals and deliverables are well-defined.

Can you work with my existing team?

Yes. My background includes working directly alongside Product, Engineering, QA and executive business stakeholders. Working cadence and repository access can be agreed before kickoff.

Can you work with confidential data?

Yes. We can establish an NDA and secure handling procedures before sharing proprietary materials. Please avoid transmitting credentials or sensitive personal data in the initial enquiry form.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the problem and the outcome you want. We can clarify the scope before deciding on next steps.
