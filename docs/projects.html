PayTrack: payment reconciliation, from BRD to UAT

Problem

Operations teams manually investigate mismatched payment records across 2 gateways, taking 48 hours to discover SLA breaches.

Role

Lead BA: Authored BRD, FRD, NFRs, 10 BDD stories, STTM and REST API sequence flows; mapped 8 requirements to 15 test scenarios.

Engineered automated 99.8% reconciliation matching logic with 5-minute exception alerting.
