Who I am

I’m Gautam Reddy, a Business Analyst with 4+ years of experience in requirements analysis and delivery, translating business needs into clear, testable functional and non-functional requirements.

What I do:

  • Elicitation & Scoping: Facilitating stakeholder workshops, discovery interviews, and backlog prioritization (MoSCoW).
  • Technical Specifications: Authoring BRD, FRD, REST API integration contracts, and BDD Given/When/Then user stories.
  • Data & Process Analysis: SQL querying, data mapping, and AS-IS / TO-BE process modelling with BPMN and UML.
  • Testing & UAT: Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM), test-case coordination, and defect triage with Engineering.
