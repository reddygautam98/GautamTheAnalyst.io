Before we begin
Frequently asked questions.
A few useful details for clients considering a first conversation.




What should I include in my enquiry?
Share the problem, who it affects, any deadline, the data or systems involved, and what you want to improve. A short description is enough to start; avoid including passwords or confidential personal data.


Do you work on fixed-scope tasks?
We can discuss a defined deliverable or discovery phase. Scope, timeline, assumptions and fees need to be agreed after the work is understood; this site does not publish unverified pricing.


Can you sign an NDA?
That can be discussed before project details are shared. Please do not send sensitive credentials, regulated records or proprietary files through this initial web form.


Does the enquiry form store my details?
No. This static website prepares an email draft in your device’s email application. It does not submit details to a website server or store them. You must review and send the email yourself.
